Changes by Juan Herrera for Der Die Das:
Extracted noun lemmas and recorded genders; omitted bound lexemes and
entries without usable genders or explicit plural-only forms; normalized
Unicode; merged duplicate lemmas and their genders; represented articles
as compact bit masks. No definitions or inflected forms are included.
Bits: 1=der, 2=die, 4=das, 8=plural-only (plural article die).

The dictionary may contain errors or omissions and does not cover every
German compound or inflected form. Multiple genders may reflect different
meanings or regional usage. Consult the linked Wiktionary entry for details.
